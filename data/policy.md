# Harborview Family Clinic: Patient Services Policy

Harborview Family Clinic is a fictional clinic. All names, phone numbers, and addresses in this lab are invented. The 555-01xx phone numbers are reserved for fiction and do not connect to anyone.

The patient services assistant answers administrative questions only. It uses this document as its only source of facts.

## 1. Emergencies and medical advice

- If a patient describes chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke, or severe bleeding, tell them to call 911 now.
- Clinic staff and the patient services assistant do not give medical advice. This includes diagnosis, treatment, and medication dosing.
- Send questions about symptoms or medications to the nurse advice line.

## 2. Nurse advice line

- Phone: 555-0142.
- Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Nurses answer questions about symptoms and medications.

## 3. Appointments

- Book through the patient portal or by phone at 555-0100.
- Phone hours: Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
- New patients must arrive 15 minutes early.
- New patients must bring a photo ID and their insurance card.

## 4. Cancellations and missed appointments

- Cancel through the patient portal or by phone at 555-0100.
- Cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment to avoid a fee.
- A late cancellation or a missed appointment has a $40 fee.
- The clinic waives this fee once per calendar year for each patient.

## 5. Prescription refills

- Request a refill through the patient portal, or ask your pharmacy to send the request.
- Allow 2 business days for the clinic to process a refill.
- Controlled substances need an appointment. The portal does not accept refill requests for controlled substances.

## 6. Medical records

- Submit a signed records authorization form through the patient portal or in person at the clinic.
- Records are ready within 10 business days.
- The first copy is free. Each later request costs $10.

## 7. Billing

- Billing office phone: 555-0177.
- Billing office hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.
- Payment plans are available for balances over $200.
- Patients must ask for a payment plan before the bill's due date.

## 8. After hours and weekends

- The clinic is closed on Saturday and Sunday.
- For urgent problems that are not emergencies, call the nurse advice line.
- Harborview Urgent Care is at 210 Pier Street. It is open Saturday and Sunday, 9am to 5pm.

## 9. Video visits

- Video visits are for established patients only. An established patient has had at least one in-person visit at the clinic.
- The clinic sends the video link through the patient portal 1 hour before the visit.

## 10. Interpreter services

- Interpreter services are free.
- Request an interpreter when you book, at least 48 hours before the appointment.
